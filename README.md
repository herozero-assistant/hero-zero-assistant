# BoltMind

Public site: https://herozero-assistant.github.io/hero-zero-assistant/

Static product site for Hero Zero automation. The same current program screenshots are shown in all six site languages: nine desktop images and eleven Android APK images in `assets/screenshots/`. Tabs, captions and platform labels are translated in `platform-copy.js`. The app interfaces shown in the screenshots are in English.

The page also presents **BoltMind Heroic Sense** as a separate, read-only mission advisor. It ranks available missions by XP per energy or coins per energy and does not automate gameplay. Its own checkout links and prices are BRL 35 on Hotmart and USD 8 on Payhip. The visual ranking motif on the page is illustrative; the BoltMind Bot screenshots and features belong only to the full bot.

Advertised lifetime prices are BRL 219 on Hotmart for Brazil and USD 41 on Payhip for USD purchases, covering PC and Android. The site displays the checkout currency instead of estimated exchange rates. Update the prices separately in Hotmart and Payhip; the site does not control those checkouts. `pricing.json`, `app.js` and `platform-copy.js` carry the displayed values. The coupon promotion has been removed; the purchase section highlights one-time payment, individual access, support, and recurring updates and fixes.

Support is Discord and email. Login and tickets are intentionally deferred at the user's request. No credentials or private customer data are collected by the site.

Previous design is preserved in Git history and branch backup/boltmind-before-real-product.
